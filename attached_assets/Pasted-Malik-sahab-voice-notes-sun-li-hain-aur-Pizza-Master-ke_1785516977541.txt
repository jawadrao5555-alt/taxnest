Malik sahab, voice notes sun li hain aur Pizza Master ke aaj raat ke records (orders, print jobs, settings) live server par check kar liye hain. Teen alag masle hain — aur aap ki baat theek hai, do inme se har company ko ho sakte hain:

1) KOT pehli dafa direct kyun nahi nikla (dine-in + takeaway dono): Pizza Master par "KDS Auto Print" ka switch ON hai. Is switch ka matlab hai: KOT cashier ki taraf se nahi, kitchen ke KDS screen ki taraf se print hota hai. Aaj raat kitchen ka KDS screen band/off tha, is liye order lagne par KOT kahin nahi nikla — manual reprint isi liye chalta raha (woh seedha printer ko jata hai). Records mein saaf dikh raha hai: order lagte waqt koi print job bani hi nahi, sirf reprint wali bani (jo kamyab hui).

Fori hal: Pizza Master ke Customize page se "KDS Auto Print" OFF kar dein — KOT phir pehle ki tarah har order par direct niklega.
Pakka hal (meri tajweez): main system smart bana doon — agar KDS screen chal nahi raha ho to KOT khud-ba-khud cashier wale purane tareeqe se nikal jaye. Is se har company mehfooz ho jayegi.
2) Takeaway ka bill kahin nahi nikla: Woh delivery/takeaway sale system mein theek lagi (PRA par bhi submit hui), lekin us bill ki print request server tak aayi hi nahi — jabke usi waqt ke aas paas baqi prints theek chal rahi thin. KOT wala hissa to upar wale KDS switch se explain ho jata hai; receipt ka na nikalna network ke lamhati jhatke ya purane fallback par girne se lagta hai. Main is par bhi hifazati intezam laga sakta hoon (agar silent print fail ho to aik dafa khud dobara koshish kare).

3) Slip ki shape aur side mein ghusna: Yeh koi kharabi nahi — proof bill ka design template aisa hi hai (kal aap hi ke kehne par Arial font laga tha). Items ke naam do line mein tootne se gap zyada lagta hai, darmiyani lines kam hain, aur content bayen taraf chapta hai. Yeh har company ko aise hi dikhega. Main isay behtar kar sakta hoon: rows tight, items ke beech dashed lines, naam ke liye zyada jagah (kam tootay), aur slip kaghaz par center.
