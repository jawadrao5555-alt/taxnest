DepartmentIrrigation DepartmentCategorySystem ImprovementSub CategorySystem ImprovementDistrictLODHRANDetailsSubject: Request for Reopening of Complaint No. 104-6878131 and Immediate Site Inspection for Shifting of Mogha No. 16475 Left (Ali Wah Minor) Basti Aaqil Wala Mouza Sandhi Wala Lodhran in Public Interest Respected Sir, I respectfully submit that my Complaint No. 104-6878131 regarding the shifting of Mogha No. 16475 Left (Ali Wah Minor) has been closed without conducting any site inspection or technical feasibility assessment. The complaint was filed purely in the larger public interest to protect the residents of Basti Aqil Wala, Lodhran City, and not for any personal benefit. The existing Mogha is located at the beginning of the residential area, due to which an open irrigation watercourse passes through the locality for approximately 825 meters before reaching the agricultural land. As a result, the residents are continuously facing serious public problems including mosquito breeding, stagnant dirty water, foul smell, unhygienic conditions, accidents involving children and elderly persons, motorcycles and other vehicles falling into the watercourse, and obstruction in daily movement. During the rainy season, these problems become even more severe and create serious health hazards for the entire locality. Instead of inspecting the site, the Irrigation Department repeatedly directed me to provide the names of irrigators, outlet details, RD numbers and submit a joint application from the irrigators. With due respect, this is neither my responsibility nor my legal duty. I am neither the Sub Divisional Officer (SDO) nor the Executive Engineer (XEN) nor an employee of the Irrigation Department. I submitted this complaint only in the public interest. The responsibility of identifying the outlet, RD, beneficiaries, ownership, irrigation record and technical feasibility lies entirely with the Irrigation Department. The Department already possesses complete official records of Ali Wah Minor and Mogha No. 16475 Left, and its technical staff is fully competent to verify all required information. Moreover, the concerned Sub Engineer, Mr. Ghulam Muhammad, is fully aware of the existing site, the present watercourse alignment and the proposed location of the Mogha. Therefore, there is absolutely no justification for directing the complainant to perform official duties that legally fall within the jurisdiction of the Irrigation Department. If the irrigators themselves were genuinely concerned about public welfare and efficient irrigation, they would have requested shifting of the Mogha long ago. The existing alignment unnecessarily carries government irrigation water through the residential area before reaching the agricultural land. This results in unnecessary wastage of irrigation water through seepage and leakage and also increases irrigation time for the cultivators themselves. The proposed shifting of Mogha No. 16475 Left by approximately 500 meters towards the canal side will eliminate approximately 825 meters of open watercourse from the residential area while ensuring uninterrupted irrigation supply to the agricultural land. This proposal will provide the following public benefits: Elimination of the open watercourse from the residential area. Protection of children, women and elderly persons from accidents. Improvement in sanitation and public health. Elimination of mosquito breeding and foul smell. Conservation of valuable government irrigation water. Reduction in irrigation time for cultivators. Easier operation and maintenance for the Irrigation Department. Continued irrigation supply to agricultural land without affecting lawful water rights, subject to technical feasibility. This matter does not require unnecessary correspondence or repeated demands from the complainant. It simply requires the concerned SDO Canal to visit the site, inspect the existing alignment, verify the ground realities and prepare a technical feasibility report. The Department should discharge its statutory responsibilities instead of shifting its official obligations onto a private citizen. I therefore respectfully request that: Complaint No. 104-6878131 may kindly be reopened. The SDO Lodhran Canal Sub Division may be directed to immediately conduct a detailed site inspection along with the concerned Sub Engineer. The Irrigation Department may itself verify all technical details relating to Ali Wah Minor and Mogha No. 16475 Left from its official record instead of requiring the complainant to collect such information. A comprehensive technical feasibility report may be prepared and submitted without further delay. If found technically feasible, Mogha No. 16475 Left may kindly be shifted approximately 500 meters towards the canal side in the larger public interest. I once again clarify that I am seeking no personal benefit whatsoever. My sole objective is to protect the residents from accidents, diseases, mosquito breeding and unhygienic conditions, conserve valuable government irrigation water, improve irrigation efficiency for cultivators and promote the larger public interest. I shall remain grateful for your kind intervention and early action.
