1.F  NestPOS product stock IN  — stock ADJUST, not a PO
-------------------------------------------------------
[VERIFIED FROM CODE]

NestPOS has NO /pos/.../purchase or /pos/.../suppliers route.

  GET|POST /pos/inventory/adjust

  docs/nestpos-book.md Baab 12

  MadadgarLocalEngine stock answer

  “Stock adjust: product chunein → Add (+) / Remove (−) / Set Exact (=)
   → quantity → (Add par purchase price) → Reason (New Purchase, …)”

So in NestPOS, “new purchase / receive stock” is a labelled ADJUSTMENT,
not a PurchaseOrder.

5. CLASSIFICATION (A/B/C/D) — BASED ONLY ON CODE
================================================================

There is no single module named Receipt Control. Split by product:

NestPOS (the Excel master target)
  Goods-in of SELLABLE products:
    C) stock adjustment mechanism
       (/pos/inventory/adjust, reason may be “New Purchase”)
    plus a SEPARATE A) inter-branch receipt workflow
       (/pos/inventory/transfer/.../receive)
    NOT B) purchase/receiving (no NestPOS PO UI)

  Goods-in of INGREDIENTS:
    C) stock adjustment only
       (opening on create + /ingredients/{id}/adjust)

  Customer bill “receipt”:
    D) print / display settings (/pos/receipt-settings)
       plus cash_received on the sale

FBR POS
  B) purchase/receiving as one-shot RECEIVED PO
     with optional C) correct-qty as adjustment_in/out on product edit
     and A) is not the primary receive path

DI panel
  B) purchase/receiving, two-step draft → receive

Health
  B) pharmacy purchase/batch receive (separate product)

If the owner’s phrase is inventory-shaped, NestPOS “Receipt Control”

6. DOES IT BELONG ON THE SINGLE-SHEET INVENTORY MASTER?
================================================================

Recommendation: NO.

[VERIFIED FROM CODE] reasons it must stay OUTSIDE the
Product / Ingredient / Recipe master import:

1. Existing NestPOS Product Excel is catalog-only. It has never imported
   stock qty, supplier, PO number, received date, or movement type.
   (PosController::downloadProductTemplate / importProducts)

2. Existing Recipe Excel is BOM-only. It find-or-creates ingredients and
   upserts product_recipes. It does not post ingredient_movements except
   indirectly if a brand-new ingredient were given stock — and the
   importer does not take opening stock as a column.
   Headers: Product Name, Product Code, Ingredient Name, Unit,
            Quantity Needed, Cost per Unit (optional)

3. Receiving (wherever it exists) is TRANSACTIONAL:
   dated, numbered (PUR-/PO-), statused, voidable, branch-scoped,
   supplier-scoped, and it writes ledgers. A master sheet is
   IDEMPOTENT catalog/BOM. Mixing them would re-receive stock on
   every re-import unless a complex “alreadyStocked” guard were copied
   from FBR product-form opening logic — which NestPOS product Excel
   does not have.

4. NestPOS product inbound and ingredient inbound use DIFFERENT
   ledgers and movement types. One “Receipt” column could not post
   correctly without a row-type that is already the job of Stock Adjust
   / Ingredient Adjust / (FBR) Receive Stock screens.

5. Receipt Display (the only owner-attributed “control … on receipts”
   feature) is a COMPANY setting, not a per-row inventory field.

6. Branch transfer receive requires an in-transit TRANSFER_OUT id.
   That cannot be expressed as a catalog row.

7. FBR purchase void, bonus qty, invoice discount, and supplier ledger
   paid_amount have no meaning on a recipe BOM line.

[INFERRED]
Opening stock on FIRST create of an ingredient (current_stock on
IngredientController::store) is the only catalog-adjacent inbound.
Even that is posted through the adjustment ledger so it is not counted
twice. Putting live Opening Stock on a re-importable master sheet is
unsafe unless the importer copies the FBR “alreadyStocked / never
duplicates on edit re-save” rule. Safer: leave opening stock to the
existing adjust screens (or a future dedicated receiving sheet).

[UNKNOWN]
Whether a LATER, separate Excel for FBR/DI purchases is desired.
That would not be the Inventory Master.


If the answer were forced YES (not recommended)
-----------------------------------------------
Minimum columns that would even be able to POST like current NestPOS
adjust (NOT FBR PO), still a bad fit on the same sheet:

  Row Type, Item Code, Qty In, Unit Cost (kharid), Branch, Reason,
  Date, Notes

That is a movement sheet, not a master. 5–10 example rows would look
like transactions (2026-09-01, Cheese 5 kg @ 1200, New Purchase) sitting
next to BOM lines (Large Pizza needs 120 g cheese). The current Recipe
Excel already proves the shopkeeper sheet is BOM-shaped, not GRN-shaped.

No example receiving rows are given as a proposed master format,
because the recommendation is to keep them out.

8. RECOMMENDED SINGLE-SHEET FORMAT (master only)
================================================================

Goal: one sheet a shopkeeper can fill for Pizza / Burger / Wings that
creates/updates:
  - pos_products (sellable dishes)
  - ingredients (raw)
  - product_recipes (BOM: qty per 1 sold unit)

Do NOT include: PO numbers, suppliers, received qty, transfer status,
receipt printer toggles, live stock, or stock-check physical counts.

Suggested headers (keep existing names where they already exist):

  A  Row Type          PRODUCT | INGREDIENT | RECIPE
  B  Product Name      (PRODUCT + RECIPE)
  C  Product Code      SKU/Barcode (TEXT) — existing Recipe + Product alias
  D  Price             PRODUCT only (menu rate)
  E  Category          PRODUCT only
  F  Description       PRODUCT only
  G  Tax Rate %        PRODUCT only
  H  Unit (UOM)        PRODUCT sale unit (existing product Excel)
  I  Tax Exempt        Yes/No
  J  Third Schedule    Yes/No
  K  Ingredient Name   INGREDIENT + RECIPE
  L  Ingredient Code   optional, ingredients.code
  M  Ingredient Unit   kg|g|ltr|ml|pcs|dozen|pack  (RecipeInventoryService::UNITS)
  N  Cost per Unit     ingredient cost_per_unit; Recipe Excel already has this
  O  Min Stock         ingredient min_stock_level (threshold, not a receipt)
  P  Quantity Needed   RECIPE only, per 1 sold unit
  Q  Active            Yes/No for product/ingredient/recipe line

Rules to preserve from current importers
  - SKU/Barcode columns FORMAT_TEXT
  - Match product: barcode → SKU → name (product Excel)
  - Match recipe product: code → name (recipe Excel)
  - Ingredient find-or-create by name+unit (recipe Excel)
  - RECIPE duplicate (product, ingredient) UPDATES quantity_needed
  - Do not seed ingredients.current_stock from this sheet on re-import
  - Company lock + quota for PRODUCT creates (product Excel already does)
  - feature:recipes requires inventory; do not import RECIPE if recipes off
  - Sample rows: keep “Misal:” (or equivalent explicit marker)

Opening stock / receiving
  Leave to /pos/inventory/adjust (products) and
  /pos/restaurant/ingredients/{id}/adjust (ingredients).
  Optional later: a SECOND sheet/file named after the real UI
  (“Stock Adjust” or, if FBR, “Receive Stock”) — never “Receipt Control”
  unless the owner names a new module in code.

10. RISKS / INVARIANTS TO PRESERVE
================================================================

[VERIFIED FROM CODE]

1. Two stock ledgers: recipe dish → ingredients; non-recipe → inventory_stocks.
   Never deduct both for the same sold line.

2. product_recipes unique (product_id, ingredient_id). Import must upsert.

3. quantity_needed is per 1 sold unit. No yield field exists — do not invent one.

4. conversion_factor is stored but unused in consume math. Do not assume
   Excel units auto-convert.

5. NestPOS product inbound is adjustment_in, not TYPE_PURCHASE.
   Do not post PUR- documents from a NestPOS master import.

6. FBR Receive Stock writes PurchaseOrder STATUS_RECEIVED + TYPE_PURCHASE
   against `products`, not `pos_products`. Do not cross-wire catalogs
   (docs/architecture/24-architectural-invariants.md: DI ↔ POS isolation).

7. Company_id on every row. Branch writes must use BranchStockService
   actorCanUse / writeBranchId. All-branches view must not silently
   dump stock into head office (FBR already refuses).

8. Cashiers cannot receive/adjust/transfer (assertNotCashier / PosAdminOnly).

9. Ingredient adjust cannot drive current_stock below zero.

10. Transfer receive is single-consumption under lockForUpdate; cannot
    receive twice; cannot receive more than sent; cancel after receive
    is forbidden (make a new transfer back).

11. FBR purchase void: only received; not if returns exist; kharid fallback
    skips voided purchases; payments are not deleted.

12. Sale munafa / recipe consume snapshots must not be rewritten by a
    later cost edit (kharid / cost_per_unit).

13. Product Excel TEXT barcodes; never CSV as the default (Pizza Master
    8.9E+12 incident — comments in PosController).

14. Recipe sample rows skipped only via explicit “Misal:” marker, never
    by inferring “Chicken Burger” is fake.

15. recipes feature depends on inventory. Master import of RECIPE_LINE
    while recipes/inventory is off would 403 at the route layer today.

16. Printed tickets remain ENGLISH; Receipt Display prefs are company
    JSON — do not store them on inventory rows
    (invariant 16; settings preservation invariant 15).

17. Stock-check Excel is a count worksheet keyed by Line ID, not a
    catalog. Do not merge it into the master.

[INFERRED]
Re-importing Opening Stock on the master would double-post
ingredient_adjustment / adjustment_in rows. That is the main reason
receiving/opening must stay off the sheet.

FINAL ANSWERS (A–E)
================================================================

A) Exact meaning of Receipt Control in current NestPOS
   [VERIFIED FROM CODE] There is no feature, table, route, or string
   named “Receipt Control”.

   Closest OWNER-NAMED receipt feature:
     Receipt Display / /pos/receipt-settings — company print toggles
     (description: “Control the address, NTN, phone & footer message
     on receipts”). This is not inventory.

   Closest NestPOS INVENTORY “receive” behaviours:
     1. Stock Adjust (/pos/inventory/adjust), often reason “New Purchase”,
        movement type adjustment_in — this is how sellable stock is
        received today.
     2. Ingredient adjust / opening kitchen stock — how raw stock is
        received today.
     3. Branch transfer receive (wasool ho gaya / received_quantity) —
        how stock is received FROM ANOTHER BRANCH.

   Closest TaxNest “Receive Stock” module (not NestPOS routes):
     FBR POS Stock & Purchase one-shot RECEIVED purchase_orders
     (PUR-…, TYPE_PURCHASE). DI panel has a draft→receive twin on the
     same tables.

   [INFERRED] In an Inventory Master conversation, “Receipt Control”
   most likely refers to goods-in. In NestPOS that is stock/ingredient
   ADJUSTMENT, not a GRN/PO document. Do not implement a new GRN
   concept under that name.

B) Whether it belongs in the single-sheet master
   NO. Keep Product / Ingredient / Recipe (catalog + BOM) on the sheet.
   Keep receiving, transfers, stock-check, supplier bills, and receipt
   printer settings on their existing screens (and existing Excels
   where they already exist: recipes, products, stock-check).

   Description, Tax Rate %, Unit (UOM), Tax Exempt, Third Schedule,
   Ingredient Name, Ingredient Code, Ingredient Unit, Cost per Unit,
   Min Stock, Quantity Needed, Active.
   Reuse current Product Excel + Recipe Excel names/aliases.

   transfer status, and receipt-printer flags.

   See section 9 (Pizza / Burger / Wings PRODUCT + INGREDIENT + RECIPE
   rows). No receiving rows.

   See section 10. Highest: two ledgers; NestPOS inbound is adjustment
