# TaxNest Distribution ERP scope register

**Status** Planned independent ERP. No complete distribution runtime, integrated PR, independent commercial deploy or acceptance is established from the #477 baseline. Existing inventory, POS and financial features are reusable, subject to the boundary and contract verifications below.

**Authority** Final approval §9 and original ecosystem brief §6. First milestone covers an end-to-end isolated industry scenario. Full scope spans FMCG, beverages, tobacco, pharma, cosmetics, food/general distribution and multi-brand, multi-principal, multi-warehouse deployments.

## Existing and reusable

`app/Services/BranchStockService.php`, `InventoryService.php`, `PosStockInService.php`, `SupplierLedgerService.php`, `HealthLedgerService.php` and `DiInvoiceInventory.php` cover narrow demonstrated capabilities. Reuse canonical UoM, inventory movements, payments, financial and regulator integration contracts through scoped adapters. Validate behaviors and dated tax decisions before reusing cross-product services. Salesflo, SAMS and SAP are documented workflow references, with no proprietary assets or assumptions about vendor support.
