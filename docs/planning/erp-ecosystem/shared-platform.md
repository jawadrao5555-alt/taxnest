## Contract boundaries

Use the existing Laravel modular monolith. Reuse tested adapters and keep native record ownership with each product. Keep risky document/backup processing, analyzer/device connections and performance-heavy reporting in isolated workers when appropriate. Cross-product dependency direction is product → shared contract. Verify behavior at source and target scopes. Each individual product can operate independently.
